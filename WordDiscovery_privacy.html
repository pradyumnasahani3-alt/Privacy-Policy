Privacy Policy — Word Discovery

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Discovery ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. Word Discovery is developed and published by Pradyumna Sahani.

Summary

Word Discovery does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal information. Your puzzle progress, coins, hints, and achievements are stored locally on your device. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which independently collects some device and advertising data as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, using Android's local database storage. This data is never sent to us or any server we operate:

  • Puzzle progress, completed levels, and stars earned
  • Coins, hint tokens, and lives balance
  • Achievements and your discovered-word collection
  • Daily Challenge streak

This data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android's App Info settings. If Android's standard device backup is enabled on your phone, this data may be included in your own Google Account backup, managed entirely by Android/Google — we do not have access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads (e.g., to earn extra hints) using Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google acts as an independent data controller for this processing.

You can review how Google handles this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings — opt out of personalized ads
  • How Google uses information from apps that use Google services

Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhy
INTERNETRequired to load banner and rewarded ads
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATELets the ad SDK check connectivity before requesting an ad
VIBRATEHaptic feedback when you find a word or complete a level — no data is collected

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children's privacy

Word Discovery is a general-audience word puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your information. Aside from the AdMob ad-serving data described above, we do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any data on our own servers — the app currently has no backend server or account system.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress data.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device's Ad Settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization) or via Google Ads Settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
