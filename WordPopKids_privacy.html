Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-23 Last updated 2026-08-23 App version 1.0.0

WordPop Kids is a flashcard learning app built for young children. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app touches, what it never asks for, and how the small amount of advertising it shows is kept appropriate for kids.

01Overview

WordPop Kids helps children learn new words through flashcards, scratch-and-reveal cards, and listen-and-choose games, organized into categories like animals, vehicles, shapes, and colors. The app is designed to work entirely on the device — there is no login, no chat, no user-generated content, and no way for a child to contact anyone through it.

This policy covers the WordPop Kids Android app (package com.ktl.talky) and applies to players of all ages, including children.

02No accounts, no sign-in

WordPop Kids does not ask a child (or a parent) to create an account, sign in, or enter a name, email address, birthdate, photo, or any other personal detail. There are no forms to fill in anywhere in the app.

03What stays on the device

To make the game work, WordPop Kids keeps a small amount of gameplay information in a local database on the device itself:

  • Which words and levels have been completed, and star ratings earned
  • Coins and badges collected through play
  • App settings, such as sound and haptic preferences

None of this leaves the device. It is never uploaded to a server, never linked to an identity, and is not accessible to us as the developer.

04Permissions we use

The app requests a short, deliberate list of Android permissions:

PermissionWhat it's for
INTERNETLoading the advertising shown between levels
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEChecking connectivity before requesting an ad
VIBRATEShort haptic feedback on correct answers and rewards

WordPop Kids does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access, and does not use the Android advertising ID — that permission is explicitly disabled in the app.

05Advertising

The app shows a small number of ads — a banner, and occasional interstitial or rewarded video ads — served through Google AdMob. These ads are configured to be treated as directed at children: no behavioral or interest-based targeting, and no use of the advertising identifier described above.

Google, as the ad-serving provider, may still process limited technical information (such as IP address and device type) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with its own advertising policies. We do not control or see this data ourselves.

06Children's privacy

WordPop Kids is built with young children in mind, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from any user, child or adult. The app contains no chat, social features, external links a child could tap into unrelated content, or ways to share data outside the device.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through some means we haven't anticipated, contact us using the details below and we will remove it promptly.

07Parent area

The "Parents" section of the app — where progress and settings can be reviewed — sits behind a simple parental gate (a short task unlikely to be completed by a young child) so that it is a caregiver, not the child, who reaches those screens.

08Clearing your data

Because all progress is stored locally, you are always in full control of it:

  • Clear it any time via Android Settings → Apps → WordPop Kids → Storage & cache → Clear storage
  • Remove it entirely by uninstalling the app

Neither action requires contacting us, since we never held a copy of this data in the first place.

09Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future update adds a new feature that touches data differently — we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.

10Contact

Questions about this policy or how WordPop Kids handles information are welcome any time.
