Vocabulario: English Spanish Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Vocabulario: English Spanish (“the app”) is an English–Spanish and Spanish–English dictionary and vocabulary-learning app. This policy explains what information the app and its service providers process.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, dictionary searches, saved words, or learning progress. Searches, saved words, practice progress, and app preferences are stored locally in the app’s private storage on your device.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display advertisements. AdMob may process device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, approximate location inferred from network information, device information, and ad interaction information to deliver, measure, and improve advertising. This information may be collected and shared by Google according to its policies.

The app does not send your dictionary searches, saved words, or definitions to advertisers. You can manage advertising preferences through Android device settings. See Google’s advertising privacy information.

Voice search and pronunciation

If you use voice search, the app passes your request to the speech-recognition service selected on your device. That service may process audio or speech according to its own privacy policy. The app does not retain voice recordings. Text-to-speech is provided by your device’s selected speech service.

How information is used

Locally stored information provides search history, saved words, pronunciation, practice, flashcards, and progress features. Ad-related information is used by Google AdMob for advertising and measurement.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. AdMob data is managed by Google under its applicable policies. See Google Privacy & Terms.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Because the app displays third-party advertising, parents and guardians should review Google’s applicable policies and the app’s Google Play audience settings.

Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising and the speech-recognition/text-to-speech services available on your device. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features, SDKs, or data practices change. The revised policy will be published at the same location with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the support email published on the app’s Google Play listing.
