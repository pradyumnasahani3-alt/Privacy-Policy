Vocabulario: English Spanish Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Vocabulario: English Spanish is an English–Spanish and Spanish–English dictionary and vocabulary-learning app.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, searches, saved words, or learning progress. These features and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Advertising is enabled in the app. The app uses Google AdMob, part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display banner and occasional interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect or share device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, approximate location inferred from network information, device information, diagnostic information, and ad interaction information for advertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. The app does not send your dictionary searches, saved words, definitions, or learning progress to advertisers.

Ads may be personalized based on applicable settings and consent. You can reset or limit the Android advertising ID and manage ad personalization in your device settings. Google’s processing is described in Google’s advertising privacy information and Google Privacy & Terms.

Voice search and pronunciation

Voice search uses the speech-recognition service selected on your device. The app does not retain voice recordings. Text-to-speech is provided by your device’s selected speech service.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. AdMob data is retained and managed by Google according to Google’s policies. You can manage or reset advertising identifiers through Android settings. Requests concerning AdMob data should be directed to Google.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, SDKs, or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support email published on the app’s Google Play listing.
